# Cursor: Pricing FAQ Steers Power Users Away from the Headline  Tier
Checked on: 2026-10-07 | Subject: Cursor (Anysphere)

## 1. Claim and Why It Matters
Cursor's public pricing table presents an individual entry price of /month, but its official documentation and FAQ explicitly direct regular daily agent users to the unadvertised  Pro+ tier. For GTM engineers, this demonstrates how multi-model agentic IDEs use decoupled compute pools to prevent margin erosion from long-context model inference.

## 2. Pricing Page
* **Tiers:** Hobby (, no card required), Pro (/mo), Business/Teams (/user/mo).
* **Stealth Tiers (Docs):** Pro+ (/mo), Ultra (/mo).

## 3. Metric
Two distinct usage pools:
1. *Cursor Models:* Internal fine-tunes and routing layers.
2. *Other Models:* Third-party frontier LLMs billed at direct API pass-through rates.

## 4. Credits and Limits
When users exceed monthly pooled allowances, Cursor triggers 'On-Demand Usage' at direct token rates. Teams tiers append a 'Cursor Token Rate' surcharge (.25/1M tokens) on external inference.

## 5. Upgrade Path
Limit dialogs and in-editor popups point users directly to higher tier pools or require an active card on file for usage overages.

## 6. Benchmark
ChartMogul's 2026 AI Churn Wave report highlights that AI-native tools priced below /month experience median Gross Revenue Retention (GRR) of 23%, whereas those priced above /month achieve 70% GRR.

## 7. What I Could Not Verify
Conversion distribution between the headline  Pro plan and the  Pro+ plan.
