# Lovable: Credits Carry Asymmetric Unit Cost Across Subscription Tiers
Checked on: 2026-10-07 | Subject: Lovable.dev

## 1. Claim and Why It Matters
A single credit on Lovable does not have a static dollar value: higher subscription tiers pay twice as much per credit unit. This illustrates a value-capture strategy where platform feature monetization is bundled into the credit price rather than broken out into separate feature fees.

## 2. Pricing Page
* **Free:** 5 build credits/day, capped at 30/month.
* **Pro:** Starts at /month for 100 credits (.25/credit).
* **Business:** Starts at /month for 100 credits (.50/credit), scaling to 10,000 credits.

## 3. Metric
*Build Credits*: Discrete units consumed by code-generation operations and agent builds.

## 4. Credits and Limits
* **Expiration Policy:** Monthly plan credits expire 2 months post-issuance. Top-up credit packs remain valid for 12 months.
* **Top-up Rates:** Pro top-ups cost  per 50 credits (.30/ea); Business top-ups cost  per 50 credits (.60/ea).

## 5. Upgrade Path
Users hitting monthly quotas cannot purchase top-up packs on the Free plan; they must upgrade to a recurring paid tier before buying additional capacity.

## 6. Benchmark
SaaS Capital 2025 benchmarks: Median B2B SaaS NRR is 101% with 91% GRR. Pure credit-based models risk higher contraction during macroeconomic budget freezes.

## 7. What I Could Not Verify
Average monthly credit burn rate per active development project.
