# Product, Pricing & Monetization Teardowns

A collection of product teardowns focused on how SaaS and AI products package usage, communicate limits and translate infrastructure costs into pricing.

The goal is to look beyond the headline price and understand the mechanics behind the customer experience: usage allowances, credit systems, limits, metering and plan design.

## What I look for

- How usage is measured and communicated.
- How plans and allowances shape customer behaviour.
- Where product experience meets infrastructure and inference costs.
- How limits, resets and overages affect perceived value.
- What the pricing model signals about the product's target customer and business model.

These are product analyses based on publicly available product information and observations. They are intended to explain the mechanics and trade-offs, not to imply access to internal company data.
